## Featured Dish 1 — 微波炉蛋糕的做法

- **Estimated cooking difficulty:** ★ (easy)
- **Main ingredients:** 黄油, 面粉, 泡打粉, 鸡蛋 (1 个), 白（红）糖, 盐 — with optional add-ins: 牛奶, 咖啡粉, 巧克力, 麦片, 坚果, 饼干屑, 香蕉
- A 2-minute microwave "ding" cake; beginners should expect up to 20 minutes.

## Featured Dish 2 — 豆角焖面的做法

- **Estimated cooking difficulty:** ★★★ (medium)
- **Main ingredients:** 鲜面条 (300g), 肉/五花肉 (100g), 豆角 (150g), 葱 (10g), 姜 (5g), 蒜 (10g), plus 生抽, 老抽, 耗油, 十三香, 鸡精, 味精, 盐
- A lazy-day one-pot favourite: beans, pork and noodles braised together.

## Featured Dish 3 — 意式烤鸡的做法

- **Estimated cooking difficulty:** ★★★ (medium)
- **Main ingredients:** 鸡腿肉 (1–2 pieces per person), 盐, 黑胡椒, 橄榄油, 蒜, 柠檬汁, 欧芹
- Oven-baked at 180 °C for 30–40 minutes, finished with lemon juice and chopped parsley.
